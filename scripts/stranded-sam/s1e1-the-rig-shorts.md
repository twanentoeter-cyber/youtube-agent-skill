# Stranded Sam — Season 1: The Rig — Episode 1 as Shorts (Day 1–7)

Narration: calm, clinical, second person ("you" = Sam). ~150 wpm.
Every Short: day counter top-left, one ask at the end.

---

## PART 1 — Day 1

**Hooks**
- A (8.5/10, false-promise / dramatic irony): "The last helicopter just left. Yours comes tomorrow morning. It never does."
- B (7/10, POV): "You're the last person on an oil rig in the middle of the North Sea."

```
[0:00–0:05] HOOK
[ON SCREEN: "DAY 01". Wide shot: a helicopter lifting off a rig helipad. Sam waves from the deck, small.]
The last helicopter just left. Yours comes tomorrow morning. It never does.

[0:05–0:10] TURN
[ON SCREEN: The helicopter shrinks to a dot. Grey sea in every direction.]
This is day one of ninety. Alone on an oil rig.

[0:10–0:20] BEAT 1 – Where you are
[ON SCREEN: Map zoom-out: rig icon, the coast far away, nothing in between.]
The rig is being shut down. The crew is gone. You stayed one extra night
to sign the final handover.

[0:20–0:32] BEAT 2 – The checklist
[ON SCREEN: Sam with a clipboard ticking boxes: generator, watermaker, radio. Green checks.]
Generator: running. Watermaker: running. Radio: working.
Everything a person needs, for one night.

[0:32–0:42] BEAT 3 – Night
[ON SCREEN: Night. Sam in a bunk, headlamp on, the rig lights glowing outside the porthole.]
You go to bed early. Pickup is at nine.

[0:42–0:48] CLOSE
[ON SCREEN: Radio on the desk. The green light flickers… then goes dark. Black.]
At 3:12 a.m., the radio goes silent. Follow for part two.
```
Runtime: ~95 words ≈ 40 s

## PART 2 — Day 2

**Hooks**
- A (8.5/10, open loop): "Every way to call for help on this rig stopped working at the same second."
- B (7.5/10, time pressure): "Your helicopter is due at nine. It's 9:40."

```
[0:00–0:05] HOOK
[ON SCREEN: "DAY 02". Sam tapping a dead radio handset.]
Every way to call for help on this rig stopped working at the same second.

[0:05–0:10] TURN
[ON SCREEN: Sam lines up three devices on a table.]
So you check them. One by one.

[0:10–0:22] BEAT 1 – Three systems
[ON SCREEN: Each device with a red X: VHF radio, satellite phone, laptop with "No connection".]
The radio. The satellite phone. The internet.
Three separate systems. All dead.

[0:22–0:32] BEAT 2 – What that means
[ON SCREEN: Sam on the deck, looking at the antenna mast. The mast is fine.]
The antennas are fine. The power is on.
If everything fails at once, the problem isn't on the rig.

[0:32–0:42] BEAT 3 – Waiting
[ON SCREEN: Clock on the wall: 9:00… 9:40… 11:15. Sam on the helipad, staring at an empty sky.]
Nine o'clock comes. Then ten. Then noon.

[0:42–0:48] CLOSE
[ON SCREEN: Sam turns back toward the rig. Text: "No one is coming."]
No helicopter. So you start counting what you have. Part three is next.
```
Runtime: ~95 words ≈ 40 s

## PART 3 — Day 3

**Hooks**
- A (9/10, surprising link): "On an oil rig, every glass of water costs you fuel."
- B (7/10, inventory): "You have food for a month. That's not your problem."

```
[0:00–0:05] HOOK
[ON SCREEN: "DAY 03". Sam holding a glass of water. A small diesel can icon appears next to it.]
On an oil rig, every glass of water costs you fuel.

[0:05–0:10] TURN
[ON SCREEN: Sam opens a storeroom door with the headlamp on.]
Day three. You take stock of everything.

[0:10–0:20] BEAT 1 – Food
[ON SCREEN: Shelves of tins, rice, pasta. A counter ticks up.]
The galley still has food. Tins, rice, pasta. Weeks of it, if you're careful.

[0:20–0:34] BEAT 2 – Water
[ON SCREEN: Diagram: sea water → watermaker → fresh water. A power cable runs from the generator into the machine.]
Water is different. The rig makes fresh water from sea water,
by pushing it through a filter. That takes power.
And power comes from the generator.

[0:34–0:42] BEAT 3 – The equation
[ON SCREEN: Simple equation on screen: WATER = POWER = DIESEL.]
So water is power. Power is diesel. And the diesel doesn't come back.

[0:42–0:48] CLOSE
[ON SCREEN: Close-up of the diesel gauge needle.]
Food isn't what runs out first. Follow for part four.
```
Runtime: ~105 words ≈ 42 s

## PART 4 — Day 4–5

**Hooks**
- A (8/10, contrarian): "The cold won't get you because you're wet. It'll get you because of what you're wearing."
- B (7.5/10, sacrifice): "To save fuel, you turn off the heating. In the North Sea."

```
[0:00–0:05] HOOK
[ON SCREEN: "DAY 04". Sam switching off a big breaker. The lights in the corridor go dark, one section at a time.]
To save fuel, you turn off the heating. In the North Sea.

[0:05–0:10] TURN
[ON SCREEN: Sam's breath visible as small white clouds.]
Now you have to stay warm without it.

[0:10–0:20] BEAT 1 – One room
[ON SCREEN: Floor plan of the rig. Everything greys out except one small room.]
You stop heating the whole rig. You live in one small room
and shut every door behind you.

[0:20–0:32] BEAT 2 – What you wear
[ON SCREEN: Two shirts side by side: cotton with a red X, wool with a green check. A water drop lands on each.]
Cotton loses most of its warmth once it's damp. Wool keeps some, even wet.
So the cotton comes off. The wool from the crew lockers goes on.

[0:32–0:42] BEAT 3 – Day five
[ON SCREEN: Night, Sam on deck. On the horizon: four tiny clusters of lights.]
Day five. At night, you can see the other platforms on the horizon.
Four of them. Still lit.

[0:42–0:48] CLOSE
[ON SCREEN: Zoom on the four lights.]
Somebody is still out there. Part five tomorrow.
```
Runtime: ~115 words ≈ 46 s

## PART 5 — Day 6–7 (episode finale)

**Hooks**
- A (9/10, countdown): "There were four lights on the horizon. Now there are three."
- B (7.5/10, question): "Why would anyone turn their lights off in the middle of the sea?"

```
[0:00–0:05] HOOK
[ON SCREEN: "DAY 06". Night. Four light clusters on the horizon. One blinks out.]
There were four lights on the horizon. Now there are three.

[0:05–0:10] TURN
[ON SCREEN: Sam sitting on the deck with a blanket and binoculars.]
So you watch them. All night.

[0:10–0:22] BEAT 1 – Signalling
[ON SCREEN: Sam's headlamp blinking toward the lights: three flashes, pause, three flashes.]
You signal with your headlamp. Three flashes. Three of anything means help.
No light flashes back.

[0:22–0:32] BEAT 2 – Day seven
[ON SCREEN: "DAY 07". Two lights left. Then one.]
Day seven. Another one goes dark. Then another.

[0:32–0:42] BEAT 3 – The thought
[ON SCREEN: Wide shot from far away: one rig glowing in total darkness. Sam's rig.]
Your rig is now the brightest thing for miles.
That's either a rescue beacon… or an invitation.

[0:42–0:50] CLOSE
[ON SCREEN: The last light on the horizon goes out. Only Sam's rig is lit. Text: "END OF EPISODE 1".]
The last light goes out. Only yours is left.
Follow, so you're here for day eight.
```
Runtime: ~105 words ≈ 44 s
